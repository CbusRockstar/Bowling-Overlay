This is the personalized LaneTalk connected Bowling-Overlay and companion control pages.

Add the hosted Bowling-Overlay.html to your OBS Scene and open the hosted control.html in a browser source.

1) Once Loaded into the Hosted Control page, Connect to LaneTalk or switch to Manual Scoring
2) Decrease or increase the number of players you wish to display
3) Enter the names of the players you wish to display
4) Enter the player's League Average
   A) If player is Absent for the night you can toggle the blind checkbox and that will provide a blind score of the player's Average minus 10 mins.
5) If LaneTalk is connected and able to display player data, then allow scoring to automatically display
   A) otherwise, select manual scoring and select the player whose turn it is.
     i) You have two options to enter scores
       I) Select Strike (X), Spare (/), or numbers (0-9)
      II) Select the number of Pins still on deck
         a) After the first ball click the button to advance to the second ball then select Spare (/), or select the pins that were knocked down
6) You also have an option to select from showing all 10 frames, or just 3 frames at a time.
  NOTE: The 3 Frame method show only show frames 1-3 for the first 3 frames, and then advance to the next active frame
7) You also have the option to compare 1 player against another.  In this mode, the active player will show if they have a Pin Lead or Pin Deficit against the player they are up against. (Note that this a scratch total meaning that league handicap is not included.)
8) You also have the ability to show the entire scorecards for all tracked players.


Future improvements might include Team vs Team Tracking, Lane Indicators and more
